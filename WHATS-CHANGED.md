# What's changed

This page lists the changes to the report on New York City Council discretionary funding (Schedule C) to arts and cultural organizations that a reader would notice: new sections, new data, changes to how awards are counted, and corrections when we published something wrong. Newest first.

- **History begins:** 2026-08-19. The report was first published on this date. Corrections made before it may be missing.
- **Data refresh:** Not on a schedule. The report uses the Council's fiscal year 2027 funding disclosure file dated July 2, 2026, saved in this repository. A newer release is used only when someone changes the report to use it.

To report a bug in this report, or to ask for a new feature, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/BetaNYC/schedule-c-org-classification/issues).

## 2026-11-12: New page listing what has changed in this report

- **Tags:** Site
- **What changed:** This page is new. It lists the changes to the report that a reader would notice. If we ever publish a number that turns out to be wrong, this page will say so plainly. A link to it will appear in the footer of every page the next time the report is rebuilt.
- **More detail:** [The change history in this repository](https://github.com/BetaNYC/schedule-c-org-classification/commits/main)

## 2026-09-04: Wider count of arts and culture awards, and totals by Council Member and agency

- **Tags:** Site, Data
- **What changed:** The report now also counts an award as arts and culture when the IRS classifies the organization that received it under "Arts, Culture and Humanities," and again counts awards paid through the Department of Cultural Affairs, which the August 20 version had left out. The count of arts and culture awards went from 1,257 to 2,253, totaling $71,484,741. New sections show how much of that funding came from individual Council Members, the Speaker and the borough delegations, and through which City agencies.
- **More detail:** [The change](https://github.com/BetaNYC/schedule-c-org-classification/commit/f996174)

## 2026-08-20: Awards counted by funding initiative only, and a first match to IRS records

- **Tags:** Site, Data
- **What changed:** The first count of arts and culture awards included every award from an arts or culture funding initiative and every award paid through the Department of Cultural Affairs. From August 20 it counts only the funding initiatives, so the count went from 1,946 awards ($62,102,457) to 1,257 awards ($39,810,000). A new section matches the organizations that received awards to the IRS list of tax-exempt organizations by tax ID.
- **Who it affects:** Anyone who copied the count of arts and culture awards on August 19 or 20, 2026.
- **More detail:** [The change to the count](https://github.com/BetaNYC/schedule-c-org-classification/commit/219d38c) · [The first IRS match](https://github.com/BetaNYC/schedule-c-org-classification/commit/c5159b1)

## 2026-08-19: The report is published

- **Tags:** Site, Data
- **What changed:** The first version of the report went online. It explains why Council discretionary funding to arts and cultural organizations is hard to total, and makes a first count of those awards for fiscal year 2027.
- **More detail:** [The report](https://betanyc.github.io/schedule-c-org-classification/) · [The change](https://github.com/BetaNYC/schedule-c-org-classification/commit/cd841c1)
