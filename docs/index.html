Schedule C Funding of Arts and Cultural Organizations

New York City Council discretionary funding to non-profits and agency initiatives (colloquially known as Schedule C funding) is a significant source of funding for cultural and arts organizations in New York City. Getting a complete view of Schedule C funding of the sector (or any single sector, by extension) is not straightforward:

  • Expense and capital funding disclosure data are released annually, by fiscal year, via Excel files on the Council’s website
  • Awards are not classified or categorized in any way.
