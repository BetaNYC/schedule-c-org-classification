Schedule C Funding of Arts and Cultural Organizations

New York City Council discretionary funding to non-profits and agency initiatives (colloquially known as Schedule C funding) is a large source of funding for cultural and arts organizations in New York City. Getting a complete view of Schedule C funding of the sector (or any single sector, by extension) is not straightforward as:

  • expense and capital funding disclosure data are released by fiscal year, via Excel files on the Council’s website, and
  • awards are not classified or categorized beyond their Source, the initiative or provision from which the funds are awarded.

There are two immediate ways to identify awards to arts and cultural organizations:

  1. The initiative or provision in Source explicitly references arts or culture
  2. The contracting city agency is the Department of Cultural Affairs (Agency: DCLA)

There are 173 funding initiatives for FY 2027. Those that explicitly apply to arts and cultural organizations are:

  • Coalition Theaters of Color
  • Cultural After-School Adventure (CASA)
  • Cultural Immigrant Initiative
  • Film Accelerator Model
  • SU-CASA
  • Support for Arts Instruction
  • Community Composting at Cultural Institutions Groups
  • Creative Arts Team

Filtering for the above two conditions returns 1,946 of 10,040 awards, a total of 19.4%. These awards comprise only 8.8% of total Schedule C funds, $62,102,457 out of $705,564,000

This approach is limited by the omission of organizations doing arts or cultural work, but whose Schedule C funding is not easily identifiable as such.
